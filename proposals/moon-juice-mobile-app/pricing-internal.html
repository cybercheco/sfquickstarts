DIFFERENT FROM THE CLOTHING BRANDS

What makes Moon Juice operationally heavier than the apparel clients.

1. Regulatory exposure

FDA / FTC supplement marketing rules. AI cannot say "treat", "cure", "prevent disease." Every claim requires citation. Brand-side legal review is non-negotiable.

2. Rx interaction handling

Customers disclose medications. AI must flag interactions and defer to physicians. We need a real interaction database and physician-confirmation workflow.

3. Subscription ops at scale

Refill management is the brand's LTV mechanism. Pause/skip/accelerate/bundle workflow needs to be calm, humane, and bulletproof.

4. Health-data adjacency

Goals, allergies, Rx, cycle data — not HIPAA but close to it under CCPA-CMIA + GDPR special category. Privacy posture must be tighter than apparel clients.

DEAL-MAKING TACTICS

How to anchor and protect the conversation.

  • Lead with compliance, not features. Most wellness apps cut FDA / FTC corners. Position our AI guardrails as the brand's protection.
  • Studio is mandatory and the Apothecary citation manager is the killer demo. Show the legal-team-approval flow. Kills competitor proposals.
  • Pre-negotiate the Enterprise upgrade path. 12–15 month timeline expected — make the upgrade fee visible early.
  • AI overage clause is non-negotiable. Apothecary chat in 3 languages with compliance pre-screening is expensive.
  • Brand-side legal review on every customer-facing claim. Get this signed in the contract before any work starts.
  • Rx interaction database licensing. Pass-through at cost (RxNorm is free; commercial APIs aren't). Make this explicit.
WHAT WE DON'T SAY OUT LOUD

Internal-only notes — sharper than apparel clients.

Reputation risk to manage

One AI hallucination claiming an adaptogen "treats anxiety" or "cures fatigue" is an FDA warning letter waiting to happen. Insist on brand-side legal review of every customer-facing claim. Budget for an in-house compliance reviewer on our team, not theirs. Walk away if they push back.

Margin target — combined

Growth tier infra+AI cost at expected MAU (~10K active in Year 1) is ~$1,400–1,800/mo. Plus Studio at ~$260/mo. At combined $4,250/mo we're at 50–60% gross before maintenance dev. Tightest margin of any client. Watch the AI line carefully — Apothecary chat is the swing factor.

Upsell sequencing

Don't propose practitioner B2B portal at the same time as Phase 1 contract. Land Growth + Studio, deliver Apothecary + Refill Vault + Ritual well, prove the compliance posture works, *then* propose Enterprise + practitioner portal as the next step.

Walk-away signals

(1) Brand pushes back on legal review of AI claims. (2) Brand wants to ship before discovery is complete. (3) Brand resists Rx interaction database licensing as cost-plus. Any one of these = walk. Wellness reputation risk is too high to absorb.
