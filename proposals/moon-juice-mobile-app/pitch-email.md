# Pitch Email to Moon Juice

> One-page outreach email. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. Length:
> ~230 words. Designed to read on a phone in under a minute.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for Moon Juice
2. mockups: an Apothecary app for Moon Juice
3. for Amanda — a Moon Juice app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:` (deceptive), all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis,
"proposal", "opportunity", "free", "limited", "urgent", "guarantee",
"cure", "treat", "miracle".

## Recipient

**Amanda Chantal Bacon** (founder & CEO) is the natural first
recipient. She's the public face of the brand, the author of the
Moon Juice Manual, and the person whose education work the app would

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I take Brain Dust every
  morning" or "your Manual changed my routine." Verifiable claims
  about Moon Juice only.
- **No medical claims, even casual ones.** No "your products work
  miracles" or "fixed my anxiety." Even compliments that imply
  efficacy can become liability if forwarded.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** A real anecdote
  ("I noticed Sephora made you a Clean brand") outperforms anything
  a stranger can write.

## Why these choices

- **Name the customer's actual question.** "Which Moon Dust is for me,
  when do I take it, can I combine it" is the pain point — opening
  with it shows you understand the brand.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.** Differentiates
  from typical agency proposals.
- **Compliance language up front** — "strict FDA / FTC guardrails,
  never diagnoses, always cites, defers to physicians." Signals that
  you understand the category's regulatory reality before they have
  to ask.
- **One link, not three.** Spam-filter friendly.
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery.** Lower commitment, higher reply rate.
- **The "either way" sign-off.** Closes without manufactured urgency.
