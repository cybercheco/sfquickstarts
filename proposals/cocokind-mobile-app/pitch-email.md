# Pitch Email to Cocokind

> One-page outreach email. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. Length:
> ~230 words. Designed to read on a phone in under a minute.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for Cocokind
2. mockups: a Facts-first app for Cocokind
3. for Priscilla — a Cocokind app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:` (deceptive), all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis,
"proposal", "opportunity", "free", "limited", "urgent", "guarantee",
"miracle", "cure", "best skincare". Cosmetic-rule sensitive language
also raises spam filter flags.

## Recipient

**Priscilla Tsai** (founder & CEO) is the natural first recipient.
She's the public face of the brand, the architect of Sustainability
Facts labelling, and the person whose intellectual rigor the app is
built around.

## Email body

```
Hi Priscilla,

Cocokind has done what almost nobody else in skincare will — published
Sustainability Facts on every product, refused vague "clean" language,
and sourced every ingredient claim. The mobile-app side of skincare
hasn't caught up to that level of rigor.

I sketched what a Cocokind app could look like — delivered as
Mobile-as-a-Service (MaaS), a monthly platform subscription rather
than a one-time build. Built around what you've already done, plus
the moments your customers don't yet have access to. The Facts
(digital Sustainability Facts label per product, citation-linked).
The Match (skin goals → routine from the existing catalog, no
invented claims). A Refill flow that closes the loop on packaging.
And a Studio chat with strict cosmetic-rule guardrails — never
diagnoses, always cites, defers to dermatologists.

Five-minute look — pitch deck, mockups, and the Studio admin concept
on one page:

  https://seysoservices.com/proposals/cocokind

If anything lands, I'd propose a two-week paid discovery: I sit with
your team, talk to ten of your customers (DTC, Target, Whole Foods),
align with your editorial team on what the AI can and can't say about
ingredients, and come back with a lockable Phase 1 scope and a
working prototype of the Facts and Routine flows in EN and ES.
Pricing is on the page above.

Either way — good luck with the work you're already doing.

[Your name]
[role · single line]
seysoservices.com
```

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I scanned the QR on a
  bottle at Target" or "your serum changed my skin." Verifiable claims
  about Cocokind only.
- **No medical claims, even casual ones.** No "your products work
  miracles." Even compliments that imply efficacy can become liability.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** Reference Priscilla's
  Gloss Angeles podcast on stopping calling the brand "clean," or the
  Sweetyhigh feature on Sustainability Facts — both are real, public,
  and signal that you read carefully.

## Why these choices

- **Lead with Priscilla's published stance** ("refused vague 'clean'
  language"). Specific, verifiable, signals respect for her
  intellectual position.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.** Differentiates
  from typical agency proposals.
- **Compliance language up front** — "strict cosmetic-rule
  guardrails, never diagnoses, always cites, defers to dermatologists."
  Signals you understand the category.
- **One link, not three.** Spam-filter friendly.
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery.** Lower commitment, higher reply rate.
- **The "either way" sign-off.** Closes without manufactured urgency.

## Deliverability checklist before sending

- Send from an authenticated business domain (seysoservices.com with

- Verify Priscilla's real address via Hunter.io free tier
  (`hunter.io/companies/cocokind.com`)
