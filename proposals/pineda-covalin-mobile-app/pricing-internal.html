THE RECOMMENDATION

Growth ($3,500) + Studio add-on ($750) = $4,250/mo at launch. Enterprise when one of four triggers fires.

A luxury, multi-region brand with serious AI ambitions cannot live on Starter. Enterprise on day one would over-charge and under-utilise. Growth is the right shape — and the Studio add-on is non-negotiable: every brand needs a way to maintain content, drops, translations, and concierge messages.

WHY ENTERPRISE IS PREMATURE

Three reasons to hold the upgrade for now.

1. White-label / multi-tenant is unused

Enterprise's headline differentiator. Pineda Covalín is one brand; multi-tenant adds zero value. Paying for it now is a tell that we over-sold.

2. Native modules aren't needed yet

Phase 1 has no AR. React Native delivers everything in scope. Native Swift/Kotlin matters when AR scarf try-on goes live in Phase 2 — not earlier.

3. SLA mismatched to launch reality

1h response 24/7 (Enterprise) is for live-revenue partners. At launch the app's downtime doesn't directly stop a sale; the web store carries that. Right level today is 4h.

4. Data residency not yet contractual

Multi-region launch is EN/ES first. EU/Asia residency rules become contractual when traffic from those regions becomes serious — give it 12 months.

DEAL-MAKING TACTICS

How to anchor and protect the conversation.

  • Lead with Growth, never Starter. If Growth feels expensive, expand the build inclusion (e.g. discovery sprint baked in) rather than dropping a tier. Starter quality would damage our reputation with a luxury brand.
  • Pre-negotiate the Enterprise upgrade path. Lock the upgrade fee at no penalty in the Year 1 contract — easier to land Growth today if Enterprise tomorrow is frictionless.
  • AI overage clause is non-negotiable. Pineda Covalín's docent will be heavily used in 6 languages. A viral Frida exhibit could 10× usage in a weekend. Cap our exposure: usage above 100K LLM calls/month at cost +20%.
  • Pre-pay discovery, not the build. $8–12K discovery sprint in week 1 is fine. Resist any pressure to pre-quote the build before discovery is done.
  • Cultural-content review is brand-side. Make the customer responsible for approving every customer-facing string of cultural content. Protects us from a translation or motif-attribution mistake.
WHAT WE DON'T SAY OUT LOUD

Internal-only notes.

Reputation risk to manage

This brand's value is authentic Mexican cultural craft. A single AI translation error involving Wixárika or Día de Muertos terminology could become a real story. Insist on human curator review for every cultural string. Budget for a Spanish-speaking QA in our team — not the brand's.

Margin target — combined

Growth tier infra+AI cost at expected luxury MAU (~5K active in Year 1) is roughly $700–1,200/mo. Plus Studio at ~$100/mo. At combined $4,250/mo invoice we're at 65–80% gross before our maintenance dev. Healthy. Watch the AI line, especially the docent in 6 languages.

Upsell sequencing

Don't propose Phase 2 features at the same time as the Phase 1 contract. Land Growth, deliver Phase 1 well, *then* propose AR try-on as the trigger for Enterprise. Earn the upgrade.

Walk-away signals

If they push back on AI overage clause + Enterprise upgrade pricing simultaneously, that's a sign they want flat fixed-fee pricing forever. We can't profitably support a multi-region luxury brand that way. Be willing to walk.
