¡Hola Sofía! You're looking at the Huichol "Peyote Vision" scarf. Want me to tell you what the symbols mean?
Yes — and translate to English for my mother-in-law, the gift recipient.
The pattern depicts a Wixárika shaman's vision. The deer represents the spiritual guide, the maize the sustenance of life. Each color carries meaning — blue for water, red for fire.Sourced from Wixárika cultural archive · brand-curated
Will the EN version be sent with the gift?
Yes — when she opens the parcel and confirms, the story unlocks in English in her own copy of the app.
