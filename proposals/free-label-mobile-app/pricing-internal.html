DEAL-MAKING TACTICS

How to anchor and protect the conversation.

  • Lead with the return-rate ROI. 6-point drop in returns on bras = real money. Fit AI funds the entire build in Year 1.
  • The Fit Finder is the killer demo. Show the rules editor in the Studio. Walk through how a 36DD wired customer maps to L cup wire-free. Differentiates from competitors.
  • Pre-negotiate the Enterprise upgrade path. Lock the upgrade fee in Year 1.
  • AI overage clause. Lab chat in 2 languages with body-shape opt-in is non-trivial cost.
  • Brand-side approval on inclusivity content. Trans/nonbinary fit guidance + plus-cup (G–L) confidence content needs editorial sign-off — protects the brand from misstep.
WHAT WE DON'T SAY OUT LOUD

Internal-only notes.

Reputation risk to manage

The brand's whole position is anti-shapewear, anti-body-shaming. One AI suggestion that reads as "slimming" or "flattering" is a viral story. Insist on brand-side review of every Lab chat reply about fit. Train the AI prompt to never use shape-modifying language.

Margin target — combined

Growth tier infra+AI cost at expected MAU (~5K active in Year 1) is ~$700–1,000/mo. Plus Studio at ~$125/mo. At combined $4,250/mo we're at 70–75% gross before maintenance dev. Healthy.

Upsell sequencing

Don't propose AR + photo fit-check at the same time as Phase 1. Land Growth + Studio, deliver Fit Finder + Drop well, *then* propose AR as the trigger for Enterprise.

Walk-away signals

(1) Brand wants "slimming" / "flattering" / "shaping" copy in the AI. (2) Brand wants fake-urgency mechanics in the Drop. (3) Brand pushes back on inclusivity content as default. Any one = walk.
