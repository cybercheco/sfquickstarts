# Pitch Email to Free Label

> One-page outreach email. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. Length:
> ~225 words. Designed to read on a phone in under a minute.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for Free Label
2. mockups: a fit-first app for Free Label
3. for Jess — a Free Label app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:` (deceptive), all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis,
"proposal", "opportunity", "free" (yes, ironic), "limited", "urgent",
"slimming", "flattering", "shaping". Body-language sensitive copy
also raises spam filter flags.

## Recipient

**Jess Sternberg** (founder & owner) is the natural first recipient.
She's the public face of the brand, the one quoted in Vancouver Mag
and Vancouver Is Awesome features, and the person whose
inclusivity-by-default ethic the app is built around.

## Email body

```
Hi Jess,

Free Label has done what almost nobody in the bra category will —
made wire-free, elastic-free, A-to-L-cup, locally cut and sewn, and
free size-swap default not perk. The mobile-app side hasn't caught
up. Most customers are still under-sizing on first try and treating
returns as failure.

I sketched what a Free Label app could look like — delivered as
Mobile-as-a-Service (MaaS), a monthly platform subscription rather
than a one-time build. Built around what you've already done, plus
the moments your customers don't yet have access to. The Fit Finder
(4-question flow that anchors to a bra she already wears, with body
comfort over body shape). The Drop (real batch counts, no fake
urgency). A Comfort Lab with care videos and plus-cup confidence
content. And a Lab chat with the free-swap workflow built in — never
"slimming," never "flattering."

Five-minute look — pitch deck, mockups, and the Studio admin concept
on one page:

  https://seysoservices.com/proposals/free-label

If anything lands, I'd propose a two-week paid discovery: I sit with
your team in Vancouver, talk to ten of your customers (cult-fan,
plus-cup, trans/nonbinary, Vancouver-local), and come back with a
lockable Phase 1 scope and a working prototype of the Fit Finder and
Drop flows in EN and FR.

Either way — good luck with the work you're already doing.

[Your name]
[role · single line]
seysoservices.com
```

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I wear the Andie every
  day" or "your bra changed my life." Verifiable claims about the
  brand only.
- **No body language.** No "slimming", "flattering", "shaping" — it's
  the brand's whole anti-position.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** Reference Jess's
  Vancouver Mag interview or the Vancouver Is Awesome feature on the
  brand — both real, public.

## Why these choices

- **Lead with the brand's own anti-position** — wire-free, A-to-L,
  locally made, free-swap default. Specific and verifiable.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.** Differentiates
  from typical agency proposals.
- **Inclusivity language up front** — "body comfort over body shape,"
  "plus-cup confidence," "never slimming." Signals you understood the
  brand before you wrote the email.
- **One link, not three.** Spam-filter friendly.
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery.** Lower commitment, higher reply rate.
- **The "either way" sign-off.** Closes without manufactured urgency.
