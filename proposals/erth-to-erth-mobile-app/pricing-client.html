THE PHILOSOPHY

You should pay for what the app delivers, not for what it might.

Most software vendors charge a flat fee that's expensive when you're starting out and a bargain once you've scaled. We've inverted it. The platform fee is light at launch — when the app is still earning your customers' trust — and grows only as active use grows.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

The platform fee covers everything.

  • Hosting & infrastructure — application servers, database, object storage, CDN, push notifications, transactional email.
  • AI features — fit assistant, recommendations, visual search, garment-passport summaries, repair triage. All caching and optimisation included.
  • Monitoring & on-call — error tracking, uptime monitoring, weekday business-hours response to critical issues.
  • OS-required maintenance — Apple and Google deprecate APIs roughly twice a year. We handle the updates so the app keeps working.
  • Security patches — dependency updates and security advisories, applied as released.
  • One small feature request per quarter — a UI tweak, a new screen, a copy change. Larger work is scoped separately.
  • Quarterly review — a half-day session covering metrics, customer feedback, and the next quarter's priorities.
FAIR TERMS

Three commitments to keep the relationship healthy.

30-day exit

Either side can end the agreement on 30 days' notice. The relationship is renewed every month by mutual fit, not by lock-in.

Your data, your accounts

Source code, customer data, AI logs all live in your cloud accounts. We have no leverage except being useful.

AI overage cap

If AI usage spikes beyond 100,000 calls per month, anything above is billed at our cost +20% — never a margin grab.

WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED

Passed through at cost or scoped separately.

  • App-store fees — Apple's $99/yr developer programme and Google's one-time $25 fee, paid on your accounts.
  • Payment processing — Shopify and Stripe fees on transactions, paid at standard rates.
  • Shipping & label APIs — for take-back, repair, and resale flows. Pass-through at cost.
  • Major new features — Phase 2 and Phase 3 work is scoped as separate engagements with their own fixed fees.
  • Marketing & ad spend — entirely yours to direct.
