# Pitch Email to The Ten Spot

> One-page outreach. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. ~230 words.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for The Ten Spot
2. mockups: a 33-location booking app
3. for Kristen — a Ten Spot app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:`, all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis, "proposal",
"opportunity", "free", "limited", "urgent", "hairless = better",
"flawless skin". Body / beauty-shaming language is off-brand AND
spam-flag adjacent.

## Recipient

**Kristen Gale** (founder & CEO) — public face of the brand,
profiled by Franchise Canada and others. Built the "anti-spa"
concept since 2006.

## Email body

```
Hi Kristen,

The Ten Spot has done what almost no beauty-bar brand has — built
33+ locations across North America with a booking model that's
faster, more honest, and more inclusive than anything in the
category. The mobile-app side hasn't caught up. Most franchise apps
are clunky single-location wrappers that ignore the multi-location
customer.

I sketched what a Ten Spot app could look like — delivered as
Mobile-as-a-Service (MaaS), a monthly platform subscription rather
than a one-time build. Built around what you've already done, plus
the moments your customers don't yet have access to. One-tap
re-booking with your favorite tech. A calendar that knows when your
wax is due. Service-specific prep ("don't shave 24 hr before") and
aftercare. A Studio that supports per-franchise pricing and RBAC
for corporate vs owners. And a Booker / Mindbody integration that
syncs with your existing system rather than replacing it.

Five-minute look — pitch deck, mockups, and the Studio admin concept
on one page:

  https://seysoservices.com/proposals/the-ten-spot

If anything lands, I'd propose a two-week paid discovery: I sit with
your team and 5 of your franchise owners, talk to ten of your
customers, audit the booking system, and come back with a lockable
Phase 1 scope and a working prototype of the booking + cadence
flows in EN and FR.

Either way — good luck with the work you're already doing.

[Your name]
[role · single line]
seysoservices.com
```

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I'm a Ten Spot regular"
  or "Jess at Leslieville is the best." Verifiable claims only.
- **No body language.** No "smooth," "flawless," "hairless." Off-brand
  for an "anti-spa" positioning.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** Reference Kristen's
  Franchise Canada profile or the brand's "anti-spa" philosophy.

## Why these choices

- **Lead with the brand's anti-position** — "33+ locations across
  North America with a booking model that's faster, more honest,
  more inclusive." Specific and verifiable.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.**
- **Booker / Mindbody language up front** — signals you understand
  this isn't a greenfield app build, it's an integration with their
  existing infrastructure.
- **One link, not three.**
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery.**
- **The "either way" sign-off.**

## Deliverability checklist before sending

- Send from authenticated business domain (seysoservices.com with SPF / DKIM / DMARC)
- Verify Kristen's address via Hunter.io free tier
- Plain text or near-plain HTML — no images, no tracking pixels
- Tuesday 8–10am Toronto time
- One-to-one cold; do not blast
