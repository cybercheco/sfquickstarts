DIFFERENT FROM PRODUCT BRANDS

What's specific to The Ten Spot.

1. Booking is the product

Not e-commerce, not content. The customer comes for the booking. App quality = booking quality.

2. 33+ locations + franchise model

Multi-location calendar, per-franchise pricing variation, RBAC for corporate vs owner. Most operationally complex Studio.

3. Booking system integration is critical

Booker / Mindbody / current system — customer's existing tools. Don't replace; sync. Scoped as separate engagement.

4. Technicians are the product

The relationship between customer and tech is what brings retention. App must surface it (with tech opt-in for photo/bio).

DEAL-MAKING TACTICS
  • Lead with operational savings. 10–15 hr/week per location of phone-bound staff time × 33 locations = $200K+/year operational savings. Justifies the build immediately.
  • Booking-system integration as separate scope. Don't bundle. Booker / Mindbody migration is its own 4–8 week project.
  • Per-franchise pricing transparency. The Studio supports +/-15% variation per location. Show that on the demo.
  • AI overage clause. Concierge chat at 33-location scale generates real volume.
  • Pre-negotiate 12-month Enterprise upgrade. Lock the upgrade fee.
WHAT WE DON'T SAY OUT LOUD
Reputation risk

The Ten Spot positions itself as "anti-spa" — comfortable, no judgment, accessible. One AI suggestion that makes a customer feel judged about her body or routine = brand-killer. Insist on brand-side review of every chat persona prompt. Train the AI to never suggest "you should also book X" — that's franchise upsell, not concierge.

Margin target

Combined infra+AI ~$280/mo. At $4,250 we're at 60% gross at expected MAU. Tight. Plan to upsell Enterprise by month 12.

Walk-away signals

(1) Brand wants AI to push franchise upsell ("you should book Y at Yorkville"). (2) Brand wants per-franchise unilateral pricing without master controls. (3) Brand wants laser/skin services positioned as medical treatment. Any one = walk.
