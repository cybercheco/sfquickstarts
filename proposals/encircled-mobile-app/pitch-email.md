# Pitch Email to Encircled

> One-page outreach email. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. Length:
> ~220 words. Designed to read on a phone in under a minute.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for Encircled
2. mockups: a multi-way app for Encircled
3. for Kristi — an Encircled app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:` (deceptive), all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis,
"proposal", "opportunity", "free", "limited", "urgent", "guarantee".

## Recipient

**Kristi Soomer** (founder & designer of the Chrysalis Cardi) is the
natural first recipient. She's the public face of the brand and the
person whose multi-way design IP the app is built around.

## Email body

```
Hi Kristi,

Encircled has done the hardest part — the Chrysalis has 8 documented
styles, the lifetime repair guarantee is real, the factories are 30km
from the studio. The mobile-app side of slow fashion hasn't caught up.

I sketched what an Encircled app could look like — delivered as
Mobile-as-a-Service (MaaS), a monthly platform subscription rather
than a one-time build. Built around what you've already done, plus
the moments your customers don't yet have access to. The Many (every
piece, every documented way to wear it). The Capsule (a trip planner
that builds packing capsules from your own closet). A one-tap repair
flow that turns "guaranteed for life" from PDF policy into a workflow.

Five-minute look — pitch deck, mockups, and the Studio admin concept
on one page:

  https://seysoservices.com/proposals/encircled

If anything lands, I'd propose a two-week paid discovery: I sit with
your team in Toronto, talk to ten of your customers, and come back
with a lockable Phase 1 scope and a working prototype of the
Multi-way and Repair flows in EN and FR. Pricing is on the page above.

Either way — good luck with the work you're already doing.

[Your name]
[role · single line]
seysoservices.com
```

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I scanned a QR at the
  Toronto studio" or "I've worn the Chrysalis Cardi for years."
  Verifiable claims about Encircled only.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** A real anecdote
  ("I noticed your factory is 30km from your studio — that's
  unprecedented in this category") outperforms anything I can
  invent.

## Why these choices

- **Open with what's true and specific** ("8 documented styles", "30km
  factories"). Avoids the consultant smell.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.** Differentiates
  from the typical "$80–130K build + monthly retainer" agency
  proposal, without sounding salesy.
- **One link, not three.** Multiple long URLs read as phishing to
  spam filters. Use one short link to the landing page on
  seysoservices.com.
- **Three artifacts behind one URL.** Lets the founder verify the
  craft before they invest a meeting.
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery, not contract.** Lower commitment,
  higher reply rate.
- **The "either way" sign-off.** Closes without manufactured urgency.
  Encircled's voice would never push.

## Deliverability checklist before sending

- Send from an authenticated business domain (seysoservices.com with
  SPF / DKIM / DMARC configured)
- Verify Kristi's real address via Hunter.io free tier
  (`hunter.io/companies/encircled.ca`)
- Plain text or near-plain HTML — no images, no tracking pixels
- Send Tuesday 8–10am Toronto time
- One-to-one cold; do not blast
