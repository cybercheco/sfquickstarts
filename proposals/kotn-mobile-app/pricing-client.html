FAIR TERMS

Three commitments to keep the relationship healthy.

30-day exit

Either side can end the agreement on 30 days' notice after the Year 1 term.

Your data, your accounts

Source code, customer data, AI logs, the impact ledger all live in your cloud accounts.

AI overage cap

If AI usage spikes beyond 100,000 calls per month, anything above is billed at our cost +20%.

Brand-side citation review

Every customer-facing provenance, impact, or maker claim is reviewed and approved by your editorial team before publish. AI generates; humans approve. Always. We never let the platform put your verifiable-provenance reputation at risk.

Maker-privacy clause

No precise GPS for farms or workshops. No real names without explicit maker consent. The app honours the people behind it.
