# Pitch Email to Ūnika Swim

> One-page outreach email. Voice: calm, specific, on-brand. Length:
> ~230 words. Designed to read on a phone in under a minute.

## Subject line — pick one

1. quick app idea for Ūnika Swim
2. mockups: an Atelier-from-couch app for Ūnika
3. for Betsy — a Ūnika app idea

Avoid: `Re:` / `Fwd:` (deceptive), all caps, `$`/`%`/`!!!`, emojis,
"proposal", "opportunity", "free", "limited", "urgent", "summer
body", "bikini body", "slimming", "flattering". Body-language
sensitive copy raises spam filter flags and is wrong for the brand
voice anyway.

## Recipient

**Betsy Campos** (founder & owner) is the natural first recipient.
She's the public face of the brand, profiled in Toronto Guardian,
blogTO, Trend Hunter, and Setting Mind. Brazilian heritage, Toronto
craft — the app is built around her practice.

## Email body

```
Hi Betsy,

Ūnika does what almost no swimwear brand does: makes-to-order in
Toronto, fits cup AAA–H, and accommodates mastectomy, ostomy, and
surgical-recovery customers without making a feature of their
bodies. The mobile-app side hasn't caught up — most brands treat
swim like fast fashion, not like the personal garment it is.

I sketched what a Ūnika app could look like — delivered as
Mobile-as-a-Service (MaaS), a monthly platform subscription rather
than a one-time build. Built around what you've already done, plus
the moments your customers don't yet have access to. The Atelier
(browse fabrics, draft a custom design, book the Yorkville
appointment from anywhere). The Order Tracker (6 stages, real photos
from the boutique, 4–6-week wait made transparent). And a sensitive-
fit profile that lives in a segregated audit-logged store — visible
only to the specialist, never to marketing AI.

Five-minute look — pitch deck, mockups, and the Studio admin concept
on one page:

  https://seysoservices.com/proposals/unika-swim

If anything lands, I'd propose a two-week paid discovery: I sit with
your team in Yorkville, talk to ten of your customers (cult-fan,
post-mastectomy, remote-Brazil, new buyer, Yorkville-local), and
come back with a lockable Phase 1 scope and a working prototype of
the Atelier and Order Tracker flows in EN, PT, and ES.

Either way — good luck with the work you're already doing.

[Your name]
[role · single line]
seysoservices.com
```

## Important — what this email does NOT do

- **No fabricated personal anecdotes.** No "I have a Ūnika one-piece
  I love" or "your boutique changed my swim life." Verifiable claims
  about the brand only.
- **No body language.** No "summer body," "slimming," "flattering" —
  these are off-brand AND insensitive given the surgical-recovery
  customer base.
- **No name-dropping mastectomy/ostomy customers.** The work is
  serious; treat it seriously. Reference it as a brand capability,
  never as a marketing hook.
- **If you want a real opener, write your own.** Reference Betsy's
  Toronto Guardian or blogTO interviews — both real, public.

## Why these choices

- **Lead with the brand's own anti-position** — wire-free, A-to-L,
  custom, surgical-recovery aware. Specific and verifiable.
- **MaaS positioning baked into the second sentence.** Differentiates
  from typical agency proposals.
- **Privacy language up front** — "segregated audit-logged store,
  visible only to specialist, never to marketing AI." Signals you
  understood the brand before you wrote the email.
- **One link, not three.** Spam-filter friendly.
- **Soft CTA — paid discovery.** Lower commitment, higher reply rate.
- **The "either way" sign-off.** Closes without manufactured urgency.

## Deliverability checklist before sending

- Send from an authenticated business domain (seysoservices.com with
  SPF / DKIM / DMARC configured)
- Verify Betsy's real address via Hunter.io free tier
  (`hunter.io/companies/unikaswimwear.com`)
- Plain text or near-plain HTML — no images, no tracking pixels
- Send Tuesday 8–10am Toronto time
- One-to-one cold; do not blast
